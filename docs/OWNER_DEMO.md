# Two-minute concept walkthrough

Open the [homepage](https://zenvarhq.github.io/creamcrust-demo/) at desktop or phone width. Start with the savoury/sweet composition and primary menu link. The wordmark is proposed; every photograph is illustrative and depicts neither CreamCrust products nor its premises.

Choose “Something crispy” to open the separate menu at Fried Chicken. Search “waffle”, clear the search, try Burgers and sort by price or name. Save an item, reload and reopen Saved. Open Details with the keyboard, then Escape to close and return focus.

Visit The Concept, Gallery and Visit. Gallery credits identify the licensed photographs. Business details and Maps/search/provider links are public references awaiting owner confirmation. There is no order submission, booking, checkout or verified operating-hours claim. Calling remains disabled during the no-contact period.

Open Menu Studio from the footer. It is a browser-local demonstration. Change a sample price, save, then refresh Menu in the same browser to see it. Export JSON, edit/import it, review and save. Reset restores the public-listing snapshot; it leaves saved items and the hide-prices preference intact. Clearing site data removes browser-local changes. Other browsers and visitors see the source snapshot. No shared CMS or production owner dashboard exists.

Before business adoption, obtain permission for the identity and genuine photos, confirm menu/prices/ingredients/allergens/availability and contact details, then separately scope any shared CMS. Public demonstration authorization does not imply café approval.
