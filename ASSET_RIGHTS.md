The `nc-burger` filename is an internal legacy slot name for the **sandwich** category photograph; it is not used to depict a menu burger. Burgers, sharing boxes, drinks and other categories without a suitable photograph use original abstract category artwork.

Every photo has full, medium and small WebP derivatives, with rights inherited from its reviewed source. They illustrate broad categories and do not promise the named item's appearance, portion, ingredients or availability.

Eight original code-authored SVGs comprise one editorial pattern, three ornaments, three category cards and a designed missing-image fallback. The retained monogram is explicitly a proposed ZENVAR wordmark. These graphics make no factual business claim. All are recorded in `content/assets-manifest.json`.

Manrope and Playfair Display are self-hosted subset WOFF2 fonts. SIL Open Font License notices and their copyright/author information are retained in `assets/fonts/manrope-OFL.txt` and `playfairdisplay-OFL.txt`.

The active manifest has 21 records with all required provenance, rights, accuracy, approval, alt-text and replacement fields. Historic stock files and the retired image register are excluded from this publishing snapshot. No old interior or staff imagery is included.

Changing a photograph requires a new source/rights review. The replacement helper marks new local replacements pending review; it never grants owner approval or removes illustrative labels. Authentic café assets remain outside this independent sprint, and no assets or permissions may be requested during the embargo.
